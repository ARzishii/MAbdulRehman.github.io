My work sits close to the operation, not above it.

I am a data analyst based in Faisalabad, Pakistan, working remotely with trading and manufacturing businesses. The data I handle is the operational kind: purchase invoices, stock records, sales listings, production runs, freight and duty. The job is to make those sources agree with each other, then turn them into reporting that pricing, purchasing and production decisions can actually run on.

AI is part of how I work rather than a separate skill on the list. I use Claude, GPT and Gemini to draft and stress-test SQL and Python, to design reconciliation workflows that replace manual cross-checking, and to build the internal tools that generate the reporting. Nothing goes out on trust: every output is validated against source data before anyone acts on it.

Alongside the analytics I build AI-powered SaaS products and automation systems for clients, from scoping and architecture through to deployment. Building the systems that produce data keeps me sharper at analysing it.
